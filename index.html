Circle Anywhere – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Circle Anywhere ("the app") lets you circle something on your screen, reads the text on your device, and helps you search or ask about it. This policy explains what the app does with your information. Contact: manifun19@gmail.com

What the app accesses

  • Screen content. Only after you allow Android's screen-sharing prompt, and only when you tap the floating bubble, the app takes one screenshot. It stays in the phone's memory and is discarded when you finish or close the card. It is never saved to storage or sent to our servers (we have none).
  • Text recognition. Text is read on your device using Google ML Kit. The screenshot is not sent to an OCR server.
  • Display over other apps. Needed to show the bubble and the circling screen.
  • Accessibility service (optional "Auto-paste"). If you turn it on in Android settings, the app can find the text box of the app you chose (for example ChatGPT or Gemini), type your text or instruction into it, and in some cases press its send/search button. It does not read, store or transmit anything else on your screen, and it works only for roughly 30 seconds after you pick a destination. You can leave it off; the app then copies the text for you to paste.
  • Notifications. Used for the "bubble is active" notice, which also lets you stop it.

What leaves your device

Only what you choose to send. When you tap a destination (a search engine, an AI assistant or a video site), the text you confirmed, or the circled picture in picture mode, is passed to that service or app. That service handles it under its own privacy policy. The app has no account system, no backend, no advertising and no analytics of its own.

Google ML Kit, which the app includes, may send limited technical diagnostics to Google as described in Google's ML Kit documentation.

What is stored on your device

  • Search history (up to 100 entries, if you keep it enabled), favourite providers and display preferences, in the app's private storage. You can turn history off and delete it in Settings.
  • A temporary copy of a picture while you share it, in the app's cache.

Android backup is disabled for the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Your control

You can stop the bubble at any time, revoke screen sharing, overlay or accessibility permissions in Android settings, clear history in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all local data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
